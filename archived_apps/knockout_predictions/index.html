Knockout Scoring

Base by round

Score mode

  • Correct advancing team: 1.0x base.
  • Correct official match score: 1.0x base.
  • Correct penalty shootout score, in the official winning direction: 1.0x base.
  • Non-draw official results can earn up to 2.0x base.
  • Penalty-shootout official results can earn up to 3.0x base.

Example: Argentina 1-1 Spain; Argentina advances 5-4 on penalties

  • Prediction 1-1, Argentina, 5-4 pens: 3.0x base.
  • Prediction 1-1, Argentina, 4-3 pens: 2.0x base.
  • Prediction 0-0, Argentina, 5-4 pens: 2.0x base.
  • Prediction 0-0, Argentina, 4-3 pens: 1.0x base.
  • Prediction 2-1, Argentina: 1.0x base.
  • Prediction 1-1, Spain, 5-4 pens: 1.0x base.

Round of 32 bonus questions

  • There are 9 Round of 32 bonus questions worth 2 points each.
  • The maximum Round of 32 bonus-question score is 18 points.
  • Bonus-question points are fixed and do not scale with knockout base points.
