Knockout Scoring

Base by round

Score mode

  • Exact regulation score and correct advancing team on a match not decided by penalties: 2.0x base.
  • Exact penalty shootout score: 3.0x base, only when the regulation draw score and advancing team are also exact.
  • Predicted penalty shootout and correct advancing team with the wrong regulation draw score: 1.5x base.
  • Exact regulation draw and correct advancing team with a wrong or missing penalty shootout score: 2.0x base.
  • Correct advancing team but wrong score: 1.0x base.
  • Regulation draw decided on penalties: 0.5x base fallback.
  • Wrong advancing team: 0.

Example: Argentina 1-1 Spain; Argentina advances 5-4 on penalties

  • Prediction 1-1, Argentina, 5-4 pens: 3.0x base.
  • Prediction 1-1, Argentina, wrong or no penalty score: 2.0x base.
  • Prediction 0-0, Argentina, 5-4 pens: 2.0x base.
  • Prediction 0-0, Argentina, wrong penalty score: 1.5x base.
  • Prediction 2-1, Argentina: 1.0x base.
  • Prediction 1-1, Spain: 0.5x base.

Round of 32 bonus questions

  • There are 9 Round of 32 bonus questions worth 2 points each.
  • The maximum Round of 32 bonus-question score is 18 points.
  • Bonus-question points are fixed and do not scale with knockout base points.
