Article 13

Equal Points and Qualification Rules

Step 1: Same-group ties

  1. Greatest number of points in the group matches between the tied teams.
  2. Superior goal difference from the group matches between the tied teams.
  3. Greatest number of goals scored in the group matches between the tied teams.

Step 2: Remaining ties

  1. Superior goal difference in all group matches.
  2. Greatest number of goals scored in all group matches.
  3. Highest team conduct score.
  4. Drawing of lots by FIFA.

Team Conduct Deductions

  • Yellow card: -1
  • Indirect red card from two yellow cards: -3
  • Direct red card: -4
  • Yellow card plus direct red card: -5
