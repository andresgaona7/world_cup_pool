Scoring Rules

Group Stage

  • Each correctly predicted group-stage qualifier earns 1 point.
  • A correctly predicted qualifier earns 1 extra point when it also finishes in the exact predicted advancing position.
  • Third-place teams only count as qualifiers if they are official best thirds.
  • A completely correct group order earns an extra 5 point bonus.
  • Each correctly selected best third-place qualifier earns 3 points. Best-third order is ignored.

Balance Target

  • A perfect group with an official best-third qualifier earns 11 points before best-third picks.
  • A perfect group without an official best-third qualifier earns 9 points because only the top two teams advanced.
  • The perfect first-round score is 148 points: 8 best-third groups x 11, 4 non-best-third groups x 9, and 8 best-third picks x 3.

Knockout Matches

  • Base values by round: Round of 32 = 4, Round of 16 = 6, quarterfinal = 10, semifinal = 16, third-place match = 14, final = 20.
  • For matches that go to extra time, the official score is regular time plus extra time. Penalty shootout goals are tracked separately and do not count toward that official score.
  • Score mode is additive: correct advancing team earns 1.0x base, correct official match score earns 1.0x base, and correct penalty shootout score earns 1.0x base.
  • A non-draw official result can earn at most 2.0x base. A penalty-shootout result can earn at most 3.0x base.
  • Penalty score credit is available only when the official match was decided on penalties. The penalty score must match in the correct team direction: if Argentina officially wins 5-4 on penalties, a prediction that says Spain advances 5-4 does not earn the penalty-score multiplier.

Futures

  • Correct champion: 20 points.
  • Correct runner-up: 15 points.
  • Correct top scorer: 10 points.
  • Favorite-team last round: 10 points for exact.
  • Ecuador last round: 12 points for exact.

Bonuses

  • Round of 32 bonus questions are worth 2 points each, with 9 questions for an 18 point maximum.
  • Round of 32 bonus-question points are fixed values and do not scale with knockout base points.
  • Each perfect knockout bonus is worth 2 points per match in the completed stage.
  • Perfect knockout winners bonus: Round of 32 = 2 x 16 = 32, Round of 16 = 2 x 8 = 16, quarterfinals = 2 x 4 = 8, semifinals = 2 x 2 = 4.
  • Perfect knockout scores bonus: Round of 32 = 2 x 16 = 32, Round of 16 = 2 x 8 = 16, quarterfinals = 2 x 4 = 8, semifinals = 2 x 2 = 4.
  • The winners bonus and scores bonus are awarded separately. Earning both awards both amounts.
  • The 3rd place and final do not award perfect knockout winner or perfect knockout score bonuses.
  • Perfect futures card bonus: 75 points when champion, runner-up, top scorer, favorite-team round, and Ecuador round are all exact.
