Scoring Rules

Group Stage

  • Each correctly predicted group-stage qualifier earns 1 point.
  • A correctly predicted qualifier earns 1 extra point when it also finishes in the exact predicted advancing position.
  • Third-place teams only count as qualifiers if they are official best thirds.
  • A completely correct group order earns an extra 5 point bonus.
  • Each correctly selected best third-place qualifier earns 3 points. Best-third order is ignored.

Balance Target

  • A perfect group with an official best-third qualifier earns 11 points before best-third picks.
  • A perfect group without an official best-third qualifier earns 9 points because only the top two teams advanced.
  • The perfect first-round score is 148 points: 8 best-third groups x 11, 4 non-best-third groups x 9, and 8 best-third picks x 3.

Knockout Matches

  • Base values by round: Round of 32 = 4, Round of 16 = 6, quarterfinal = 10, semifinal = 16, third-place match = 14, final = 20.
  • Winner-only mode: a correct advancing team earns 1x the round base value; a wrong advancing team earns 0.
  • Score mode: exact regulation score and correct advancing team earns 2.5x base.
  • Score mode: exact regulation score with the wrong advancing team earns 1.5x base.
  • Score mode: correct advancing team with the wrong score earns 0.5x base.

Futures

  • Correct champion: 80 points.
  • Correct runner-up: 50 points.
  • If the champion and runner-up are reversed, the entry earns 35 points instead of the champion or runner-up points.
  • Correct top scorer: 60 points.
  • Favorite-team last round: 35 points for exact, 15 points if off by one round.
  • Ecuador last round: 40 points for exact, 18 points if off by one round.

Bonuses

  • Perfect knockout winners bonus: 50 points.
  • Perfect knockout scores bonus: 200 points.
  • Perfect futures card bonus: 75 points when champion, runner-up, top scorer, favorite-team round, and Ecuador round are all exact.
